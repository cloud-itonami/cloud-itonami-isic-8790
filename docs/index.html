Other residential care activities

A forkable, governed open-source business for residential-care operators. This actor automates resident intake, safeguarding assessments, mandatory-reporting-obligation screening, background-check screening, and care-plan/incident-response finalization—each step verified by an independent Residential Care Governor and backed by an immutable audit ledger. Run independently on your own infrastructure, keep resident data under your control, and never surrender records to a closed SaaS.
